Medical Specialty: Infectious Diseases

Description:
This folder contains medical knowledge related to infectious diseases and infections caused by viruses, bacteria, parasites, or other infectious agents.

Purpose:
The documents in this folder are used by the Agentic AI medical information system as a knowledge source for symptom analysis and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

Conditions Covered:

* Common Cold
* Dengue
* Influenza
* Malaria
* Typhoid

Role in the System:
When the Specialty Router identifies infectious diseases as the most relevant medical domain, the RAG system can retrieve information from this specialty-specific knowledge base.

Note:
The information is intended for educational and research purposes only. The system does not provide confirmed medical diagnoses or replace professional medical evaluation.
