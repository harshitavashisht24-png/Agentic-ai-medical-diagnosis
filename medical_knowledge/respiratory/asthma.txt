Disease: Asthma
Medical Specialty: Respiratory

Asthma Fact Sheet
Updated: 28 April 2026

Overview:
Asthma is a chronic lung disease affecting children and adults. It involves inflammation and narrowing of the airways, which can make breathing difficult.

Common Symptoms:
- Persistent cough
- Wheezing
- Shortness of breath
- Difficulty breathing
- Chest tightness

Symptoms may become worse during respiratory infections or after exposure to certain triggers.

Possible Triggers:
- Dust
- Smoke
- Fumes
- Pollen
- Animal fur and feathers
- Strong soaps
- Perfume
- Weather changes
- Respiratory infections

Risk Factors:
Asthma can be more likely in people with a family history of asthma or other allergic conditions such as eczema and allergic rhinitis.

Diagnosis:
Symptoms can occur in several different conditions, so proper clinical evaluation by a healthcare professional is important for diagnosis.

Management:
Asthma can be controlled through appropriate medical management and avoidance of relevant triggers. Inhaled medicines can help control symptoms.

Important Note:
This information is intended for educational and research purposes. It does not provide a confirmed diagnosis or individualized treatment recommendation.
